HOW TO PREVIEW
1. Extract the ZIP folder.
2. Open index.html in a modern browser.
3. Internet access is needed for Google Fonts and remote Unsplash photographs.

IMPORTANT PLACEHOLDERS / VERIFICATION NEEDED
1. Replace temporary stock photography with approved Kaei photos where available.
2. Add Kaei's actual menu items, descriptions, sizes, and EGP prices. The current menu uses category-level descriptions and intentionally does not invent product prices.
3. Confirm opening hours; the supplied Google listing indicated 24 hours, but this should be verified before publication.
4. Confirm dine-in and drive-through availability.
5. Verify the exact Google Maps pin/address. Current links search for Kaei and the supplied address, rather than claiming verified coordinates.
6. Add official phone, email, and social media links. None have been invented.
7. The contact form is a front-end preview and DOES NOT send messages. Connect a real form service or email endpoint before publishing.
8. The current site has no online ordering, payments, reservation system, or menu-management dashboard. These require integrations and business decisions.
9. Connect the domain kaei.shop through its registrar/DNS and a hosting provider. This folder does not publish the site by itself.
10. Before launch, test all pages, links, mobile layouts, image licensing, accessibility, SEO metadata, HTTPS, and backups.

DESIGN
Palette: warm cream, espresso brown, caramel, and beige.
Typography: Playfair Display + DM Sans (loaded from Google Fonts).
Photography: Unsplash image URLs are used as temporary remote images; internet access is required to display them.
